Experience Kerala From the Water

Welcome to Karunya Tours, your gateway to the beautiful backwaters of Alappuzha, Kerala.

We offer memorable experiences through peaceful houseboat cruises, traditional shikara rides and authentic canoe journeys through the scenic waterways of Kerala.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends or someone special, we are here to help you discover the beauty, culture and peaceful charm of the Kerala backwaters.
